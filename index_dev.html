Café heatmap

During my travels I noticed a correlation between the density of cafés and how lively the neighborhood is. This map helps me find nice areas in cities I travel to. Maybe I bump into my next favorite place with the help of it once?

  • Cafés
  • Libraries

The data is a snapshot of OpenStreetMap, extracted with QLever and served as static tiles, so it is fast, but not live. It's not accurate, but it gets the job done.
